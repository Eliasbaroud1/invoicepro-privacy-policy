Delete Your InvoicePro Account

App: InvoicePro · Developer: Barood Works

This page explains how to delete your InvoicePro account and the data associated with it. You can request deletion from inside the app, or by contacting us directly.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open InvoicePro on your device.
  2. Sign in with the account you want to delete.
  3. Tap Settings in the bottom navigation.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted. This action is irreversible.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or you cannot access your account, email us from the address you used to sign up:

info@barood.cc

Please include the email address associated with your InvoicePro account in the message body. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion by reply email.

What gets deleted

  • Authentication record — your email address, sign-in identifier (Google or Apple ID), and authentication tokens stored by our authentication provider (Supabase) are permanently deleted.
  • Local app data — when you use the in-app deletion option, all locally stored data on your device is also wiped: business profile, clients, invoices, financial records, app settings, and templates.

What is retained, and for how long

InvoicePro is a local-first application. We do not collect or retain your invoices, clients, or financial data on our servers — there is nothing of that kind to retain.

If you have enabled Google Drive or iCloud backup, the backup file is stored in your own cloud account and is not automatically removed when you delete your account. To delete the backup file, go to your Google Drive or iCloud Drive and delete the InvoicePro backup folder manually.

Note: some authentication logs (sign-in events, IP address, timestamps) may be retained by our authentication provider for up to 90 days for security and fraud-prevention purposes, after which they are permanently deleted. No personal data is retained beyond this period.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You don't need to delete your entire account to remove specific data. Within the app, you can:

  • Delete individual invoices, receipts, clients, or items at any time.
  • Disable cloud backup at any time from Settings → Backup & Restore.
  • Wipe all locally stored data without deleting your account by reinstalling the app and skipping the restore step.

Questions?

Email info@barood.cc with any questions about account or data deletion.
