Delete Your InvoicePro Account

App: InvoicePro · Developer: Barood Works

This page explains how to delete your InvoicePro account and the data associated with it.

InvoicePro does not use a sign-up form. The first time you open the app, an anonymous account is created automatically so the app can run your free trial and subscription — we never collect your name, email address, or a password.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open InvoicePro on your device.
  2. Tap Settings in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted. This action is irreversible.

Because the account is anonymous and tied to your device, deleting it from inside the app is the most reliable method.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, you can email us at info@barood.cc.

Since accounts are anonymous, they are not linked to an email address. Uninstalling the app already ends your session, and the anonymous account holds no information that can identify you. We will answer any deletion question within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Anonymous account record — the anonymous account and authentication tokens stored by our backend provider (Supabase) are permanently deleted.
  • Subscription & trial state — the record of your InvoicePro Pro status, plan, and trial dates is deleted.
  • Local app data — when you use the in-app deletion option, all data stored on your device is also wiped: business profile, clients, invoices, financial records, app settings, and templates.

What is retained, and for how long

InvoicePro is a local-first application. We do not collect or retain your invoices, clients, or financial data on our servers — there is nothing of that kind to retain.

  • Device identifier — the device identifier used to grant a single free trial is retained in our trial ledger to prevent the trial from being reset by reinstalling the app. It is not linked to your identity and holds no personal information.
  • Cloud backup files — if you enabled Google Drive or iCloud backup, the backup file is stored in your own cloud account and is not automatically removed. To delete it, open your Google Drive or iCloud Drive and delete the InvoicePro backup file manually.
Note: request metadata (IP address, timestamps) may be retained by our backend provider for up to 90 days for security and fraud-prevention purposes, after which it is permanently deleted. No personal data is retained beyond this period.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You don't need to delete your entire account to remove specific data. Within the app, you can:

  • Delete individual invoices, receipts, clients, or items at any time.
  • Disable cloud backup at any time from Settings → Backup & Restore.
  • Wipe all locally stored data without deleting your account by reinstalling the app and skipping the restore step.
