Getting started

  • Open InvoicePro and tap Get Started — there's no sign-up. You're ready to create your first invoice right away.
  • Set up your business profile (name, logo, currency) in Settings so every invoice looks like yours.
  • Add a client, build an invoice from your price book, preview the PDF, and send it by email or WhatsApp.

Subscriptions & billing

  • InvoicePro is free to try. Pro unlocks unlimited invoices, quotations, and receipts.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • To restore a previous purchase, open the app's upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Your data & backups

  • Your business, client, and invoice data is stored on your device. Turn on optional iCloud or Google Drive backup (Settings → Backup & Restore) to keep a copy in your own account.
  • See our Privacy Policy for how your information is handled.

Frequently asked

  • Do I need an account? No — InvoicePro works without any sign-up.
  • Can I use my own currency and language? Yes — InvoicePro supports any currency and 6 languages.
  • How do I send a receipt? Mark an invoice as paid and a receipt is generated automatically.
